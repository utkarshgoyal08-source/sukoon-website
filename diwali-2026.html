The New Collection

Five Chapters of Light

This year, we created a collection inspired by the many little rituals and moments that make Diwali feel like home. Baithak for the card table, Rangrez for colour, Aangan for clay, Utsav for the sweets and the sparklers, and Mukta for the quiet after.

Every piece is thoughtfully made in small batches, and every candle is hand-poured using soy wax and scented with carefully chosen natural fragrances and essential oils.
